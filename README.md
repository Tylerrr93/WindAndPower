# Wind and Power: A Wayward Mod
## Overview
Wind and Power is a mod for the survival game Wayward that introduces a sophisticated, dynamic wind system and renewable energy generation. This project expands the base game's crafting and survival loop by allowing players to harness the power of the wind to produce kinetic energy.

This mod is a testament to my ability to develop complex game systems using C# programming and to create compelling new assets using 3D modeling and texturing. The project demonstrates a holistic approach to mod development, from conceptualizing new mechanics to designing and integrating the assets that bring them to life.

## Features
Wind and Power adds several new systems and items to the game, all working together to create a cohesive energy production pipeline:

1. Dynamic Wind System
Functionality: A realistic, ever-changing wind system that affects the power output of windmills. The wind's strength and direction are dynamic, creating a constant challenge for players to optimize their power generation.

Programming Highlight: This system required significant C# coding to implement. The code handles the generation of randomized, yet persistent, wind data. It calculates how wind strength impacts the rotational speed of windmills and manages the transfer of that energy to turbines.

2. Windmills
Functionality: A multi-block structure that captures wind energy. Windmills will rotate at varying speeds depending on the current wind conditions, providing a direct visual representation of the wind's power.

Design Highlight: The windmill assets were created using 3D modeling and texturing. I designed the sail, a custom multi-block base, and the rotating mechanism. The visual feedback of the spinning sails is a critical part of the mod's user experience and a key showcase of my graphic design skills.

3. Kinetic Turbines
Functionality: A new type of crafting station that converts the kinetic energy from a nearby windmill into a usable resource. Players can then use this resource to power other modded or base-game devices.

Programming & Design Highlight: The turbine is another custom asset that I designed and created. Its integration with the windmill system required intricate C# code to manage the energy transfer and storage. This demonstrates a deep understanding of game logic and the ability to link disparate systems together.

## Installation & Usage
Installation:
Download the latest release of the Wind and Power mod from the releases page.

Place the .zip file into your Wayward Mods folder.

Launch Wayward and ensure the Wind and Power mod is enabled in the Mod Manager.

Usage:
After installing, you will find new recipes for crafting the windmill components and the kinetic turbine.

Craft and place the windmill. It will begin to spin automatically based on the in-game wind.

Craft and place a kinetic turbine nearby to convert the wind energy.
